### BGP Community Guidelines by Thomas Maurer, 20221109 ###
###########################################################

Disclaimer:
This document shows an example, how BGP communities can be implemented in your network. However, the best implemention for you may still need some kind of individual adoption to fit your needs.
This is not a best practice document, rather than a document showing only one out of many options on how this can be done.

#############################################################################################

*** General Guidelines

 - ASN:2xxxx are customer readable communities
 - ASN:66x, ASN:3xxxx and ASN:4xxxx are customer writeable communities

#############################################################################################

*** Blackholing

ASN:666    Blackhole
ASN:661    Blackhole only inside originated country
ASN:662    Blackhole only outside originated country
ASN:663    Blackhole only inside originated continent/region
ASN:664    Blackhole only outside originated continent/region
65535:666  Blackhole RFC7999

#############################################################################################

*** Traffic Engineering

ASN:30011  Set Local Preference Low
ASN:30012  Set Local Preference High

ASN:40001  Prepend ISP ASN once when advertising to all ASNs
ASN:40002  Prepend ISP ASN twice when advertising to all ASNs
ASN:40003  Prepend ISP ASN thrice when advertising to all ASNs

ASN:4XXX1  Prepend ISP ASN once when advertising to Peer XXX (see Origin Peer communities)
ASN:4XXX2  Prepend ISP ASN twice when advertising to Peer XXX (see Origin Peer communities)
ASN:4XXX3  Prepend ISP ASN thrice when advertising to Peer XXX (see Origin Peer communities)

#############################################################################################

*** Origin Types, incl. Reject on specific Origin Types

ASN:20102  Origin Type Private Peering
ASN:20103  Origin Type IXP

ASN:30101  Reject on Type Downstream
ASN:30102  Reject on Type Private Peering
ASN:30103  Reject on Type IXP
ASN:30104  Reject on Type Upstream
